Why I Love This Cartoon

Stitch is super funny, wild, and strong, while Lilo is creative, brave, and kind-hearted.

Even though Stitch was created to destroy things, Lilo teaches him how to be good and care about others.

Their friendship proves that no matter how different you are, true friends always stand by you.

Famous Quote: "Ohana means family. Family means nobody gets left behind or forgotten."
